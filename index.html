About

I am a Robotics M.Eng. student at University of Maryland researching vision-language-action models at GAMMA Lab. With 2+ years of industry experience, I build autonomous systems that perceive, plan, and learn in real-world environments.

My expertise spans perception, motion planning, and hardware deployment across quadrupeds, mobile manipulators, and inspection robots. I work with SLAM, Nav2, embedded systems, and sensor integration across ROS/ROS 2 stacks.

Passionate about bridging classical robotics with learning-based methods. I am seeking a Summer / Fall 2026 internship to drive innovation in autonomous systems and real-world robot deployment.
